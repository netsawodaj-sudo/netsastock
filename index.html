NetsaStock is built specifically for streamlined automotive and three-wheeler spare parts inventory tracking. Engineered for store managers and family-operated businesses.

🏢 About NetsaStock

Operating under the official NetsaStock Company umbrella, this web app is a specialized, intelligent digital warehouse designed specifically for motorcycle and three-wheeler spare parts management (TVS King, Hora RE, Boxer).

📖 User Manual

1. Stock View: Filter by TVS King, Hora RE, or Boxer. Expand rows to Sell, Restock, Edit, or Delete parts.
2. Adding Parts: Click "➕ Add Item" and fill in all mandatory fields to save directly to Firebase.
3. Analytics & Returns: Click "📊 Analytics" to track revenue and profits. You can edit past sales prices or process returns directly from the sales ledger.
